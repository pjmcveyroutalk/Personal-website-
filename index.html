ABOUT

Technology matters. Understanding people matters more.

Over the past five years, I have worked across digital technology, finance and online products. Along the way, I learned that the hardest part of building something useful is often not the technology itself. It is understanding the problem clearly enough to know what should actually be built.

That is the role I bring to Routalk Build. I work between the person with the idea and the technology required to make it real. I listen, ask questions, simplify what is complicated and help turn the goal into a direction a designer or developer can execute.

I care about how something looks, but appearance alone is not the standard. The finished experience should help people understand the business, trust what they are seeing and know what to do next.

Whether the project starts as a rough idea, an outdated website or a workflow that is causing problems, the starting point is the same: understand it first, then build with purpose.
