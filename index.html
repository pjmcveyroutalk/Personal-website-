ABOUT

Technology matters. Understanding people matters more.

For the past five years, I have worked across the digital and financial landscape, developing my skills in communication, technology, problem solving and bringing complex ideas down to earth.

Today, that experience comes together through Routalk Build. My role is to understand the person or business in front of us, identify what is getting in the way and help shape a digital solution that feels clear, useful and real.

I am not interested in building something just because it looks impressive. The goal is to create something that helps people understand who you are, what you do and what they should do next.
