CONCEPT 01 · VETERINARY / ANIMAL CARE

Make the next step easier to understand.

The starting point was not simply how to make a veterinary website look newer. The question was how quickly someone could understand the available care, find the right service and know what to do next.

On a phone, those decisions become even more important. Important care paths, appointments and client resources should not compete with unnecessary navigation or visual clutter.

The concept explores a clearer hierarchy that brings services, appointments and useful client tools closer to the decisions visitors are already trying to make.

01

What I noticed

Important information can become harder to find when every service and navigation choice is given the same visual weight.

02

The opportunity

Create clearer care paths and make common next steps easier to reach, particularly for mobile visitors.

03

The direction

Simplify the hierarchy, organize services around visitor needs and move appointments and client tools closer to the action.

CONCEPT STATUS

Independent exploration. Not commissioned client work.

The visual concept will live here alongside the thinking behind it. The purpose is not to imply a completed project. It is to show how an existing experience can be studied and translated into a possible new direction.

CONCEPT 02 · LANDSCAPING / SEASONAL SERVICES

Design around the whole business, not one season.

Landscaping businesses often serve customers across very different parts of the year. The digital experience should make those seasonal changes understandable instead of presenting the business as if it does the same thing every month.

The concept looks at a clearer journey between landscaping, seasonal services and the actions customers or job applicants need to take.

It also explores how an online employment path could reduce friction for businesses that experience seasonal hiring demand.

01

What I noticed

A website can undersell a business when its structure reflects only one part of what the company actually does throughout the year.

02

The opportunity

Create a clearer seasonal journey for customers while also making employment opportunities easier to discover and apply for.

03

The direction

Organize the experience around seasonal needs, clear service paths and a structured online hiring workflow.

CONCEPT STATUS

Independent exploration. Not commissioned client work.

The final concept presentation will pair the visual direction with the reasoning behind the customer journey and seasonal workflow.

CONCEPT 03 · NONPROFIT

Help people understand the mission — and act on it.

A nonprofit website has to do more than explain what an organization is. It needs to help people understand why the work matters and where they fit into that mission.

The concept explores how clearer storytelling and stronger navigation could connect visitors more directly with impact, services, donations, volunteering and other ways to participate.

The direction is built around making the organization feel organized, credible and easy to understand without losing the human purpose behind the work.

01

What I noticed

Strong work can become difficult to appreciate when impact, programs and ways to help are scattered across the experience.

02

The opportunity

Connect the mission more clearly to the actions visitors can take to support it or benefit from it.

03

The direction

Create a clearer path between impact, services, donations, volunteering and involvement.
