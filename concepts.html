CONCEPT 01 · VETERINARY / ANIMAL CARE

Make trusted local care easier to navigate.

The starting point was not simply how to make a veterinary website look newer. The question was how quickly a pet parent could understand the practice, see the animals and services it supports and know what to do next.

Verona Veterinary Medical Service already has something valuable: a long-standing local identity and a broad approach to companion-animal care. The opportunity was to make that experience easier to understand at a glance while preserving the familiarity and trust of the practice.

The concept therefore focuses on clear service discovery, prominent appointment actions, useful client tools and a responsive experience that carries the same priorities from desktop to mobile.

01

What I noticed

A trusted local practice has more to communicate than a list of services. Its history, range of care and relationship with the community should be visible immediately.

02

The opportunity

Help pet owners quickly identify relevant care while bringing high-value actions such as appointments and client tools closer to the beginning of the experience.

03

The direction

Preserve the practice's established identity while creating clearer service paths, stronger hierarchy and a more useful mobile experience.

THE VISUAL DIRECTION

Verona Veterinary Medical Service.
Local care made easier to navigate.

Rather than replacing the character of an established local practice, this direction organizes that identity around the things pet owners need most: understanding available care, finding useful client tools and reaching the next action quickly.

01

DISCOVERY

Make the range of care immediately understandable.

The experience gives pet owners direct visual routes into services and the types of companion animals the practice supports instead of making them search for basic information.

02

TRUST

Let the practice's history support the experience.

The local-care message, established history and breadth of veterinary services become visible parts of the first impression rather than details buried deeper in the site.

03

UTILITY

Put useful actions where people can find them.

Appointment requests, new-client access and client tools are treated as core parts of the experience so common tasks require fewer steps on both desktop and mobile.

CONCEPT STATUS

Independent exploration.
Not commissioned client work.

This concept demonstrates one possible direction based on the practice's existing identity and positioning. It is presented as design thinking, not as a completed or commissioned client project.

CONCEPT 02 · LANDSCAPING / SEASONAL SERVICES

Design around the whole business, not one season.

Landscaping businesses often serve customers across very different parts of the year. The digital experience should make those seasonal changes understandable instead of presenting the business as if it does the same thing every month.

The concept direction will connect landscaping, winter services and customer actions while also exploring an online employment workflow for businesses with seasonal hiring demand.

This section will intentionally use a different interaction model from the veterinary concept: more operational, seasonal and task-driven rather than care-path driven.

01

What I noticed

A site can undersell the company when its structure reflects only one season or one part of the business.

02

The opportunity

Let customers move naturally between seasonal services while giving potential employees a clear digital application path.

03

The direction

Season switching, service routing, quote actions and hiring tools become the center of the experience.

CONCEPT 02 · NEXT

Seasonal operations.
A completely different experience.

The landscaping visual will not reuse the veterinary composition. Its layout and functionality will be built around changing seasons, service demand, quoting and hiring.

CONCEPT 03 · NONPROFIT

Help people understand the mission — and act on it.

A nonprofit website has to do more than explain what an organization is. It needs to help people understand why the work matters and where they fit into that mission.

The concept direction will use storytelling and proof of impact to connect visitors more directly with services, donations, volunteering and other ways to participate.

This section will intentionally feel more editorial and human than either the veterinary or landscaping concepts.

01

What I noticed

Strong work can become difficult to appreciate when impact, programs and ways to help are scattered across the experience.

02

The opportunity

Connect the mission directly to the actions visitors can take to support it or benefit from it.

03

The direction

Use impact storytelling, human context and clear participation paths instead of a traditional service-oriented layout.

THE THINKING COMES FIRST

A concept is a direction, not a claim.

These explorations exist to show how I think about business problems, communication and digital experiences. When a real project begins, the direction changes with the actual business, audience, constraints and objectives.

01

Start with what exists

Understand the business, audience and where the current experience creates friction or leaves opportunity unused.

02

Build around the actual problem

The visual language, structure and functionality should change with the business instead of forcing every project into the same template.
